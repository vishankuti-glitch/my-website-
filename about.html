🎮 About the Game

Catch the Box is a simple browser mini game. Your goal is to click the moving box as many times as possible within 30 seconds.

How to Play

  • Click the Start Game button.
  • Find the red box.
  • Click the box to earn points.
  • The box moves after every click.
  • Try to get the highest score before time runs out.
